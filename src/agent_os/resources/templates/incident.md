# Incident review

Status: draft. Owner, source, and review are required.

Use **Causal debugging** in the selected AgentOS `docs/PROCESS.md` (installed
`resources/docs/PROCESS.md`, located with `agentos resources`). Link the existing
work journal rather than creating a competing incident status record.

## Impact

[REQUIRED] Identify the affected user action, expected/actual result and observable
success/failure criterion, with facts and sources.

## Timeline

[REQUIRED] Provide facts, sources, decisions, and limitations.

## Cause

[REQUIRED] Separate hypothesis, observation, confirmed conclusion and unknown.
Link the reproduction, baseline and compact experiment journal defined in PROCESS;
preserve scope limitations and an unsuccessful reproduction explicitly.

## Actions

[REQUIRED] Record correction/mitigation, its verification stage defined in PROCESS,
rollback, outstanding evidence and the next owned experiment or verification.
