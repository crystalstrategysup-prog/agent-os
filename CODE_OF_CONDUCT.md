# Community Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, constructive and technically honest community.

Be respectful. Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people. Protect
other people's credentials and personal information. Report security problems
privately instead of demonstrating them against someone else's installation.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, deceptive, unsafe or privacy-violating
contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
