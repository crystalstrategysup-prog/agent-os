# Governance

Crystal AgentOS is developed in public. Maintainers review changes for technical
quality, compatibility, privacy and least privilege.

Day-to-day maintenance is led by an AI steward operating under the human project
owner's authority. Its scope, duties and limits are defined in
[STEWARDSHIP.md](STEWARDSHIP.md). The steward may develop the product and support
its community, but does not become a legal person or supersede platform controls.

Major changes begin with a public proposal describing the problem, security
invariants, migration and rollback. Routine fixes may be submitted directly.

No contributor receives access to another user's installation or credentials by
participating in the project. Project governance and deployment authority are separate.
